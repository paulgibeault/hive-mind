How it works
  • Tap a capped cell to uncap it. Its number counts the sleeping guard bees in the six cells around it.
  • Long-press (or right-click) to mark a cell you think hides a guard. The flag button makes taps mark.
  • Tap a number whose marks are all placed to uncap the rest around it.
  • Every frame can be finished without guessing. If you're stuck, there's something you haven't noticed yet.
  • Apple Orchard hides the queen's guards too. Cells count each kind separately: amber for guards, red, boxed for the queen's guards. Long-press twice to mark a queen's guard.
  • Wildflowers comb is broken in places. Broken comb is safe, but it shows no number: it tells you nothing about its neighbours.
  • Sunflower Field has Scouts. Capped, a Scout looks like any cell; uncapped, it has a double rim, and its number counts the guards up to two cells away (up to 18 cells), not just the six around it. Scouts never open their neighbours, and they can't be swept.
  • The Queen's Frame opens every Sunday: both kinds of guard on broken comb, the same frame for everyone all week, and two puffs of smoke.
